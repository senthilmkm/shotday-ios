Questions

Straight answers.

Is Shotday free?

Yes. Shot, food, symptom, dose, refill, history, and weight tracking stay free. Shotday Pro is optional: 14-day trial, then $4.99/month or $29.99/year for concierge, reports, milestones, and alerts.

Where is my data stored?

Locally, on your iPhone, in the app’s encrypted sandbox. There is no Shotday server. Nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app, the data is gone.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Shotday → Cancel. Or inside Shotday: Settings tab → Subscription → Manage subscription. Apple handles payment. We never see your card.

I switched phones. Can you restore my log?

We can’t — we don’t have a copy. Migrate with an encrypted iCloud or Finder backup of the old phone, which restores local storage. An on-device export is in Settings today.

I’m not on Ozempic / Wegovy / Mounjaro / Zepbound.

Enter a custom drug name and dose. The dose ladder is built for semaglutide and tirzepatide; logging, symptoms, protein, and refills still work for other GLP-1s.

Does Shotday give medical advice?

No. It is a tracking tool, not a medical device. Always consult your prescribing physician before starting, stopping, or changing any medication. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, find the Shotday charge, and tap Request a Refund.

Notifications aren’t showing up.

iOS Settings → Notifications → Shotday. Turn on Allow Notifications, Lock Screen, Notification Center, and Banners. Check Focus modes if alerts are silenced.
